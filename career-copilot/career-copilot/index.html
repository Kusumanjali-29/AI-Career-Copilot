Career Copilot is an AI-powered student career guidance platform designed to help you navigate the complex world of tech careers — from identifying your strengths to building a personalized roadmap to your dream job.

🎯

Personalized Guidance

Every recommendation is tailored to your unique skills, interests, and goals.

🤖

AI-Ready Platform

Built to integrate with AI APIs (OpenAI, Gemini) for even smarter insights.

🚀

Action-Oriented

We don't just tell you what to learn — we give you the exact steps to get there.

🔮 AI Integration Ready

Career Copilot is architected to connect with AI APIs. To enable real-time AI-powered analysis, connect an OpenAI or Gemini API key in the settings. Once connected, the platform will provide:

  • ✅ Real-time career match scoring using GPT
  • ✅ Personalized advice based on your resume text
  • ✅ Dynamic project idea generation
  • ✅ Interview question coaching
  • ✅ Cover letter and LinkedIn profile drafting
